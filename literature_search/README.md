# TMS Training Platform Literature Search

Search date: 2026-05-29

This folder is the starting evidence map for a TMS technician education platform. The first pass is intentionally broad: it captures patient-facing education topics, technician-facing equipment workflow topics, and adjacent measurement technologies such as EEG, qEEG, TMS-EEG, and fNIRS.

## Product Evidence Lanes

1. Mechanisms and neuroplasticity: how TMS, rTMS, theta burst, and deep TMS are thought to modulate cortical excitability, networks, and plasticity.
2. Safety, side effects, and patient sensations: headache, scalp discomfort, tapping sensations, seizure risk, hearing protection, syncope, mania, tolerability, and adverse event counseling.
3. Patient experience and adherence: dropout, acceptability, expectancy, treatment comfort, patient education, and protocol adherence.
4. Medication, sleep, caffeine, and state dependence: clinical and neurophysiology literature relevant to consistent caffeine, sleep, medications, alcohol/nicotine, and excitability state.
5. Technician equipment and localization: motor threshold, coil positioning, coil orientation, DLPFC localization, Beam F3, neuronavigation, stimulation intensity, and operator training.
6. Dosing and protocol variants: high-frequency, low-frequency, bilateral, theta burst, accelerated, and deep TMS protocols.
7. fNIRS, EEG, qEEG, and biomarkers: adjunct monitoring, target engagement, predictors of response, and future platform expansion.
8. Guidelines, consensus, reviews, and clinical depression protocols: high-level sources that can anchor training modules and patient FAQ answers.

## Inclusion Bias

The first-pass search is inclusive. It intentionally keeps reviews, trials, protocols, observational studies, mechanistic human studies, and relevant technical papers. Animal-only papers are excluded in the PubMed query. Later screening should remove irrelevant TMS acronym collisions, non-training-relevant papers, and papers outside clinical or technician education scope.

## Current Outputs

Generated by `scripts/collect_pubmed_tms_literature.py`:

- `pubmed_query_counts.csv`: exact PubMed queries, translations, search dates, counts, and search URLs.
- `pubmed_pmids_by_lane.csv`: deduped PMID membership across topic lanes.
- `pubmed_records.csv`: compact article metadata for deduped PubMed records.
- `pubmed_reviews_guidelines.csv`: review, guideline, meta-analysis, and consensus-heavy subset for fast synthesis.
- `pubmed_queries.json`: machine-readable query definitions.

Generated by `scripts/screen_tms_literature.py`:

- `screened_records.csv`: complete first-pass screen of every PubMed record with module assignments, priority, decision, and screening reason.
- `claim_extraction_sources.csv`: priority 1-2 sources selected for claim extraction.
- `targeted_extraction_sources.csv`: priority 3 sources retained for targeted follow-up extraction.
- `screening_summary.md`: screening counts and caveats.

Generated by `scripts/fetch_pubmed_abstracts.py`:

- `pubmed_anchor_abstracts.csv`: abstracts and MeSH terms for curated anchor sources.

Generated by `scripts/build_tms_claims.py`:

- `evidence_claims.csv`: structured source-linked claims for patient FAQ, technician workflow, mechanisms, safety, protocols, and future measurement modules.
- `patient_faq_claims.md`: patient-facing answer draft organized by module.
- `technician_workflow_claims.md`: technician-facing SOP/training claim draft.
- `claim_source_coverage.md`: claim counts by module/evidence strength and PMID coverage check.

## Next Search Sources

PubMed is only the open first pass. For a true scoping or systematic review, add institution-access exports from:

- Embase
- PsycINFO
- Cochrane CENTRAL and Cochrane Reviews
- Web of Science or Scopus for citation chaining
- IEEE Xplore or ACM Digital Library for equipment, signal processing, and fNIRS/qEEG tooling
- ClinicalTrials.gov for active and completed protocol evidence
- FDA device documents and manufacturer manuals for operational constraints

## Synthesis Plan

1. Screen `pubmed_reviews_guidelines.csv` first to identify anchor papers and module claims.
2. Screen `pubmed_records.csv` by lane for patient FAQ claims and technician workflow claims.
3. Create claim-level evidence tables with columns for claim, source, study type, population, protocol, finding, caveat, and training-module destination.
4. Convert stable claims into technician-facing scripts and patient-facing FAQ answers.
5. Mark weak, theoretical, or protocol-specific claims clearly so the platform does not overstate evidence.
